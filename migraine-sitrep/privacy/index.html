MIGRAINE SITREP · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Developer: Paradise Entertainment of NW FL LLC

Privacy at a glance: Migraine SITREP is designed to keep headache and migraine information on your device. It does not require a developer-operated account or upload episode records to a Paradise Entertainment server. Optional Apple Health access requires your permission.

1. About this policy

This policy describes how Migraine SITREP, an iOS headache and migraine tracking app developed by Paradise Entertainment of NW FL LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information. The app is intended for personal health recordkeeping, including documentation that a user may choose to share with a healthcare professional or in connection with a benefits claim.

2. Information you enter

The app may process information you choose to enter, such as headache and migraine episode start and end times, severity, symptoms, potential triggers, impacts on activities, notes, and related tracking preferences. These records can be sensitive health information.

As designed, this information is stored on your device rather than in an account or cloud database operated by us. We do not receive your migraine records as part of normal app use.

3. Apple Health

If the app offers Apple Health integration and you enable it, the app may access or write the specific health-data categories identified in Apple's permission prompts. You can decline or change these permissions in your device's Apple Health settings. Data managed by Apple Health is subject to Apple's platform controls and your device settings. We do not use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or sale.

4. Sharing and exports

If you use an export or share feature, you decide whether to send a report or record to a clinician, representative, agency, or other recipient. Once you share information outside the app, the recipient and selected sharing service may handle it under their own practices. Review exported reports before sharing them.

5. Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

Migraine SITREP is not designed to display advertisements or use developer-operated analytics to collect migraine records. We do not sell users' health information. Apple's App Store, device operating system, and optional Apple Health services operate under their own privacy practices. Any future change involving developer or third-party data collection will require an updated disclosure and policy.

6. Storage, backups, and security

We do not operate a service that stores your migraine history. Your device security settings help protect locally stored records. Depending on your device and backup configuration, some app data may be included in backups managed by Apple; Apple Health data may have separate syncing and backup behavior. We do not control those services. Use a passcode and the privacy protections available on your device.

7. Deleting information

Deleting records through available app controls removes them from the app's local records according to the app's behavior. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app container, but may not remove copies you previously exported, information retained in device backups, or records already written to Apple Health. Apple Health entries can be managed separately through the Health app. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device.

8. Children

Migraine SITREP is not directed specifically at children. Parents or guardians should supervise health-recordkeeping by minors where appropriate. The app's App Store age rating indicates content suitability, not medical appropriateness.

9. Medical and VA disclaimer

Migraine SITREP is a personal tracking and documentation tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. It does not determine eligibility for Department of Veterans Affairs benefits or guarantee any claim outcome. Migraine SITREP is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in app features, data handling, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current published version.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Paradise Entertainment of NW FL LLC at developer@paradiseentertainmentnwfl.com.
