MIGRAINE SITREP · APP SUPPORT

Migraine SITREP Support

Built for Veterans. By a Veteran.

Migraine SITREP is an iPhone migraine and headache journal designed to help people document episode timing, symptoms, and effects on daily activities. This page provides help with using the app and contacting its developer.

Need assistance? Email developer@paradiseentertainmentnwfl.com. Include your iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please do not email medical records or sensitive health information unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start or end a migraine episode?

Open Migraine SITREP and select Start Migraine to begin logging an episode. Use My Events to review an existing episode and record its end when appropriate. Add severity, symptoms, and other details as you know them.

Why does an episode say “Timed Out (unconfirmed)”?

The app can automatically time out an episode after a configurable period (six hours by default). A timeout is an administrative marker, not confirmation that the migraine ended at that time. Open the episode to review and correct its actual end time when known.

Can I edit an existing episode?

Yes. Open My Events, select the episode, review its details, and save any corrections. Only record information that accurately reflects what happened.

How do I view or export a report?

Select Reports to view your monthly SITREP and available export options. Exported files may contain sensitive health information; review them and choose recipients carefully.

Can I use Siri?

Migraine SITREP provides Siri-related actions. Available spoken phrases depend on Siri and device settings. You can use Apple's Shortcuts app to create a personal shortcut using an available Migraine SITREP action, then name that shortcut with a phrase you prefer.

Where are my records stored?

Migraine records are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device data storage. The app does not require a developer-operated account or upload your episode history to a Paradise Entertainment server. Optional Apple Health access is subject to your permission. Read the Migraine SITREP Privacy Policy for more details.

What if something is not working?

  1. Confirm that you are using the latest available app version.
  2. Close and reopen the app, then retry the action.
  3. Contact support with the steps that produced the issue and any error message. Screenshots are helpful only if they do not expose information you wish to keep private.

Important: Because your episode history is stored on your device, do not delete or reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step without first understanding the potential loss of locally stored records.

Medical and VA disclaimer

Migraine SITREP is a personal recordkeeping tool, not a medical diagnostic or treatment service. It does not establish that one condition caused another, determine eligibility for VA benefits, or guarantee a claim outcome. Migraine SITREP is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Seek professional medical advice for medical concerns.
